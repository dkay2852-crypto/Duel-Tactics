How to Play

  1. Buy monsters from the shop with gold. Reroll the shop for 2 gold.
  2. Place up to 5 monsters on the field from your bench. Sell extras if needed.
  3. Buy 3 copies of the same monster to fuse them into a stronger ★★ version.
  4. 2+ monsters of the same type grant a type bonus (e.g. Dragons +300 ATK).
  5. Start Battle — monsters clash left to right. Higher ATK wins; leftover damage hits Life Points.
  6. Earn gold after each round. Reach 0 LP and the duel is over.

Defeat

Your Life Points hit zero.
