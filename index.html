How to Play

  1. Pick Classic or Original cards, then begin.
  2. Buy from the shop (reroll costs 2 gold). 3 copies fuse into ★★.
  3. Tap a bench monster, then tap a hex on your half to place it (max 5). Tap a placed monster to return it to the bench. Drag works too.
  4. 2+ of the same Type grant an ATK bonus.
  5. Press FIGHT — units move to the nearest foe and attack. HP = DEF (min 500), damage = ATK ÷ 4.
  6. Surviving enemies deal Life Point damage (200 × Level each). Earn gold after the round. 0 LP ends the duel.

Defeat

Your Life Points hit zero.
