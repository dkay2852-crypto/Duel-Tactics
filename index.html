How to Play

  1. Travel the Duelist Kingdom island map — about 12 stops from the shore to Pegasus's castle.
  2. Stops: Duels (nameless), Bosses (Weevil→Rex→Mai→Keith→Panik), Rival Kaiba (twice), Grandpa's Shop, and Rest.
  3. Some paths branch — pick left or right. Tap upcoming stops to preview the foe and their monsters.
  4. Your LP carries across the island (start 4000). Rest restores 1000 LP. Early nameless duels cannot drop you below 1000 LP.
  5. Shop, fight, and fuse as usual. Beat Pegasus to win the run. After one win, a secret Exodia stop appears.
  6. Progress auto-saves — use Continue to resume on the same map spot.
