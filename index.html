How to Play

  1. Pick Classic or Original cards, then begin.
  2. Buy from the shop (reroll costs 2 gold). 3 copies fuse into ★★.
  3. Board size = your level (1–9). Buy XP for 4 gold, or earn 2 XP each round.
  4. Traits: unique Types on the board activate at 2 / 4 / 6. Tap a trait for details.
  5. Tap your level to see shop odds. Lock the shop to keep offers between rounds.
  6. After rounds 2, 5, 9, and 13, pick an Equip Spell. Tap an item, then a monster (max 3 each).
  7. Climb the duelist ladder — beat Yami Yugi to win the run.
  8. Press FIGHT — units slide, lunge, and cast abilities. Use 1x/2x speed.
  9. Tap a token for full stats. Drag tokens to place. Earn interest and streak gold.
