How to Play

  1. Pick Classic (API) or Original cards, then begin.
  2. Buy monsters from the shop with gold. Reroll costs 2 gold.
  3. Place up to 5 monsters on the field from your bench.
  4. 3 copies of the same monster fuse into a stronger ★★ version.
  5. 2+ monsters of the same Type grant an ATK type bonus.
  6. Battle clashes left to right. Higher ATK wins; leftover damage hits Life Points.
  7. Earn gold after each round. 0 LP ends the duel.

Defeat

Your Life Points hit zero.
