How to Play

  1. Welcome to Duelist Kingdom — start with 2 Star Chips.
  2. Win a duel: +1 Star Chip. Lose: −1. Hit 0 chips or 0 LP and the run ends.
  3. Reach 10 Star Chips to unlock Pegasus's Castle.
  4. Between rounds, tap the glowing island spot. Each area boosts matching Types by +30% ATK.
  5. Buy, fuse (3→★★), place on hexes, then FIGHT for a DUEL!
  6. Board size = your level (1–9). Lock the shop to keep offers between rounds.
  7. Tournament path: Weevil → Rex → Mako → Mai → Keith → Bonz → Panik → Castle (Kaiba, Yugi, Pegasus).
  8. Tap a token for stats. Drag to place. Items draft after rounds 2, 5, 9, 13.
